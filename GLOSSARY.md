# Pillagers

Pillagers models named residents and the domestic groups and shelter around them.

## Language

**Persona**: A persistent person whose identity and history survive changes of work, household and residence. Resident is the player-facing term for a persona living in a settlement.

**Winter**: The calendar year from one winter to the next. A person's age in winters is the elapsed number since their birth Winter.

**Family**: A kin group connected through parentage and partnership.
_Avoid_: Household

**Household**: A domestic group of residents who live together.
_Avoid_: Family

**Residence**: The shelter a household lives in, including a house or a tent.

**Building**: Persistent physical infrastructure, independent of the household occupying it.

**Food**: Whole units of the settlement's food stock.

**Materials**: Whole units of the settlement's construction and craft stock.
