# Fjordside social encounters (#48)

Meshy residents reserve exclusive pairs when their approaching routes predict a personal-space conflict. They stop before contact, turn toward each other and play Talk_Passionately / Listening_Gesture for five simulation seconds. The annual pause freezes both movement and encounter time. Destinations remain unchanged. A three-second cooldown and pair separation requirement prevent immediate repeat encounters.

MovementSystem owns navigation and pair reservations. MeshyHuman owns animation playback and existing crossfades. Legacy bodies without social clips use avoidance only. Busy residents and third parties sidestep or yield. Continuous segment checks prevent stepping across another resident's configured radius. Spawn and persona replacement also respect personal space and release old encounters.

Passing residents plan a committed entry and exit around a neighbour before reaching personal-space contact. Both sides are checked against the entire terrain corridor in 0.1 m samples, so a building corner cannot appear clear just because the waypoints are outside it. The passing clearance is twice the resident radius plus a 0.2 m margin; the look-ahead adds speed multiplied by prediction time. Eligible social pairs retain encounter priority. Encounter prediction follows the current waypoint, and a temporary terrain or passing waypoint does not disable social eligibility.

If neither side has a clear entry, residents search a full circle of escape directions, including a 180-degree turn. They finish the short escape waypoint before reconsidering the original destination. Turning still happens in place until the movement direction is sufficiently aligned with the body, and every actual step retains the continuous personal-space check. Speed, turning speed and encounter timing are unchanged.

Terrain steering also probes the intended route for speed multiplied by prediction time (currently 1.5 seconds, or 0.975 m). When that corridor is blocked, the resident selects the smallest clear turn in 22.5-degree increments and commits to its temporary waypoint before checking the destination again. This starts the turn ahead of a building rather than at its edge.

In the existing Debug panel, **Grid, axes and movement probes** shows green forward lines for clear corridors, red lines for blocked corridors, and blue lines for committed detours. These optional lines follow the current body heading; route planning checks the intended heading. Hidden helpers perform no probe rendering work.

Tuning is centralized in residentMovementConfig (radius, speed, conversation distance/duration, cooldown, separation, prediction and turning speed). The default radius is 0.48 m. The world debug metrics show state, partner and cooldown; canvas data-interactions also contains the configured radius. No rigid-body physics, group conversations or pathfinding rewrite is introduced.

Validation: deterministic head-on encounter timing/facing/route restoration; busy sidestepping; third-party avoidance; paused time; persona replacement; ten residents for 120 simulation seconds with pair ownership and separation checks; passing a waiting neighbour beside a wall; withdrawing from a crowded corner and resuming the destination without backwards walking; approaching social pairs still meeting; steering before reaching a building; social encounters during terrain steering; existing navigation and Meshy runtime tests; browser smoke check in generated Fjordside and production build. The approved test boundary is the public MovementSystem.update() interface and the existing visible Fjordside controls. Run the browser follow-up with `node scripts/qa/check-resident-passing-browser.mjs` (QA_ORIGIN defaults to http://127.0.0.1:5182).
