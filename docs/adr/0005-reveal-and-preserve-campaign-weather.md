# Reveal Winter weather before work and preserve campaign rules

Accepted for #43. Weather is a saved campaign option, enabled by default for new campaigns and disabled for older saves; disabling it introduces no weather rolls or effects, preserving their RNG stream. Determine and reveal each region’s weather at the start of its Winter, but retain that outgoing Winter’s weather through its complete annual resolution before revealing the next, so players can prepare and boundary effects cannot silently use a new forecast.
