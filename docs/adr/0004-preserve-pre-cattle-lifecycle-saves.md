# Preserve pre-lifecycle cattle save rules

Accepted for #42. Landing version 3 stores cattle reproduction and mortality rules explicitly. Version-1/2 saves migrate to disabled reproduction and zero cattle mortality, preserving their static herd and RNG stream. New campaigns enable the agreed prototype rules. Lifecycle phases run at the Winter boundary: mortality before consumption and calving after final Farmyard reconciliation. Newborns do not face mortality on their birth boundary.
