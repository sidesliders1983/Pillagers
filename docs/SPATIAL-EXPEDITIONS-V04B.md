# Spatial Expedition Groups v0.4B — #148

This opt-in `/fjord-play?discovery=1` prototype extends the existing reusable Core groups with typed physical Explore Area orders. It does not enable exploration by default or replace historical four-region Recon, Surveillance and Pillage missions. Ordinary residents still receive no individual movement orders. Physical rival presentation remains #149; spatial raids remain #150.

## Player flow

Open **Menu → Expedition groups**, enter a group name, expand **Members**, select eligible named residents and create the group. The group can be reused and edited when home. Choose **Explore Area**, tap a land destination, review the planned round-trip Food and tick estimate and select **Send group**. The pale objective ring marks only the chosen point. Cancel target has no cost; **Return home** after departure recalls the party along its actual travelled route.

Residents retain existing work-age, caregiver, availability and exclusive-membership rules. Away members stop home labour and use the existing absence rules for annual consumption, families, careers and care. They remain the same named/DNA-shaped entities in 3D. Their daily Living World movement controller does not move them while on an expedition.

## Owner-approved experimental values

- Speed: 2 metres per canonical tick.
- At the target: 25 ticks, followed by automatic return.
- Food: 1 per member for each started 125 planned ticks, including both travel legs and the target stay, paid at departure.
- A rejected/no-route order mutates nothing. Recall refunds no Food, and does not erase confirmed discoveries.
- Explore Area introduces no extra random casualties or weather speed/risk coefficients. Existing natural mortality still applies; all members dying ends the mission as Lost. Previously purchased provisions cover the trip even if home stocks later reach zero. Historic Recon/Surveillance/Pillage rules are unchanged.

Policy is saved in the optional version-1 `world.spatial` extension. A 200 m outward route at 2 m/tick takes 100 + 25 + 100 = 225 ticks: two provisions per member. The Core, not animation or camera position, owns progress and all outcomes.

## Terrain, visibility and presentation

Planning uses bounded deterministic A* over the existing final walkability grid, cardinal steps, native canonical height queries, dry ground and a maximum 0.65 local grade. Search expands at most 40,000 nodes; an exhausted/unreachable request fails clearly and asks for a nearer goal. It does not promise a route across water or implement new boat missions. Existing household/reserved foundation footprints and the middle place are excluded from new routes. No new terrain classification, terrain flattening or saved second heightfield is introduced.

Dispatch stores the validated route but reveals none of it. Confirmed progress refreshes the existing clan-specific observation mask at 8 m distance intervals and phase changes. Every refresh combines the settlement/landing with all active parties; at most 16 parties can explore simultaneously. Departed terrain remains mapped, while current visibility follows actual observers. The offer exposes an explicitly qualified planned-round-trip estimate; it exposes no route geometry, hidden terrain classification, rival names, residents or inventory.

The render layer interpolates only between confirmed route distances, follows that same route around corners and samples canonical ground height. Real members travel in a bounded single-file formation; it never predicts future Core ticks. Pause, camera changes, frame rate, culling and hidden tabs cannot advance a mission or reveal terrain. Saving during a visual fade preserves committed knowledge.

## Save/load and headless replay

Core JSON saves route, target, membership, provision charge, policy, phase, distance and timestamps. The authoritative blueprint is reattached by `FjordCampaign` after the existing versioned geography is regenerated. A bound geography context is mandatory to advance an active physical mission. Core-only tooling must provide the same context explicitly; it must not silently advance against unrelated geography. Import checks identity, route topology and canonical walkability before committing. Historical saves without `world.spatial` retain their previous semantics and gain no invented missions.

Departure and return have factual events and participant references. Food provisions appear as spending in the existing Clan Book factual ledger. Mission reports list current phase/outcome and the original charge; no looted resources or fictitious rival encounters are generated by Explore Area.
