# Spatial Expedition Groups v0.4B — #148

This opt-in `/fjord-play?discovery=1` prototype extends the existing reusable Core groups with typed physical Explore Area orders. It does not enable exploration by default or replace historical four-region Recon, Surveillance and Pillage missions. Ordinary residents still receive no individual movement orders. Physical rival presentation remains #149; spatial raids remain #150.

## Player flow

With nothing selected, choose **Create exploration group** in the contextual management panel. Core creates an empty reusable **Group 1**, **Group 2**, etc. Select a resident on the island, choose a group and use **Add to group**. A resident can **Leave group** while home. Clear the selection to see each group's expandable membership, status, **Explore Area**, **Return Home** and latest provision record. Menu → Expedition groups is a shortcut to this no-selection view. Empty groups cannot depart; active groups cannot change membership.

Choose **Explore Area**, then tap a land destination. That one tap immediately plans the complete group journey, checks and charges the provisions, and sends the group from its members' current positions. There is no additional Send group or Move group step. The group panel records the Food packed; the objective ring marks its actual destination until return, recall or a new order. Before a destination is chosen, **Cancel target** has no cost.

If the tap lands on an unavailable navigation cell, Core can choose safe walkable ground within 8 m on the same local surface. It never shifts the target across water or a steep ridge. If no valid destination/route exists, or provisions are insufficient, no Food is charged, the current journey (if any) stays intact and the player can choose another spot. **Return Home** recalls every member over their own reached route without a Food refund.

**Explore Area remains available while Traveling, Exploring or Returning**, including with a paused clock. Use it before tapping the replacement destination; ordinary ground taps remain navigation. Core takes away residents' origins from their confirmed saved positions, ignoring any rendered-origin values in a replacement command. It replaces the active destination atomically, retains the original home origins and confirmed discoveries, and plans a homeward leg. Redirected returns finish on safe navigation ground near each original departure (within 12 m), rather than treating the new departure point as home. Return Home can recall the redirected party from its newly reached positions. An active report displays Returning rather than prematurely calling a recalled journey finished. While paused, the panel explicitly says to press Start to move; issuing an order never advances time or starts the clock.

Each accepted replacement purchases new provisions for the new outward leg, target stay and planned return home, using the same per-member/125-tick rule. Previous provisions are not refunded. The saved mission record accumulates all provision charges, while each departure/change-of-course event records only its additional Food charge for the factual ledger. Redirecting creates no fictitious homecoming. A bad target or insufficient Food leaves the old order, stocks and discovery unchanged.

Residents retain existing work-age, caregiver, availability and exclusive-membership rules. Away members stop home labour and use the existing absence rules for annual consumption, families, careers and care. They remain the same named/DNA-shaped entities in 3D. Their daily Living World movement controller does not move them while on an expedition.

## Owner-approved experimental values

- Speed: 2 metres per canonical tick.
- At the target: 25 ticks, followed by automatic return.
- Food: 1 per member for each started 125 planned ticks, including both travel legs and the target stay, paid at departure.
- A rejected/no-route order mutates nothing. Recall refunds no Food, and does not erase confirmed discoveries.
- Explore Area introduces no extra random casualties or weather speed/risk coefficients. Existing natural mortality still applies; all members dying ends the mission as Lost. Previously purchased provisions cover the trip even if home stocks later reach zero. Historic Recon/Surveillance/Pillage rules are unchanged.

Policy is saved in the optional version-1 `world.spatial` extension. A 200 m outward route at 2 m/tick takes 100 + 25 + 100 = 225 ticks: two provisions per member. For individual routes, the longest outward journey determines the common provision estimate: early arrivals wait at the target, and the 25-tick stay begins when everyone has arrived. All members carry provisions for that complete group schedule. The Core, not animation or camera position, owns progress and all outcomes.

## Terrain, visibility and presentation

Planning uses bounded deterministic A* over the existing final walkability grid, cardinal steps, native canonical height queries, dry ground and a maximum 0.65 local grade. Search expands at most 40,000 nodes; an exhausted/unreachable request fails clearly and asks for a nearer goal. It does not promise a route across water or implement new boat missions. Existing household/reserved foundation footprints and the middle place are excluded from new routes. Each resident starts at their exact current rendered ground position, captured in the dispatch command and saved as the first route point. A sampled safe connector joins that position to the existing navigation grid. A resident already within a parcel may exit that containing parcel; the connector cannot cross another reserved footprint. No new terrain classification, terrain flattening or saved second heightfield is introduced.

Dispatch stores the validated route but reveals none of it. Confirmed progress refreshes the existing clan-specific observation mask at 8 m distance intervals and phase changes. Every refresh combines settlement/landing observers with the actual confirmed positions of living expedition members, deduplicating coincident observers. At most 16 parties and 62 residents can explore simultaneously, keeping the existing 64-observer discovery budget including the two founding observers. Departed terrain remains mapped, while current visibility follows actual observers. The offer exposes an explicitly qualified planned-round-trip estimate; it exposes no route geometry, hidden terrain classification, rival names, residents or inventory.

The render layer interpolates only between confirmed route distances, follows that same route around corners and samples canonical ground height. It spreads each coalesced update over the 500 ms campaign-update cadence (adapting to observed active intervals up to 1 s), rather than covering a whole batch in one 180 ms tick and stopping between updates. This adds no extra Core calls, predicted travel, saved clock or canonical movement speed change; pause freezes presentation progress. Each real member follows their own route with the native Run clip; the run animation does not increase the configured canonical speed. Early arrivals idle at the destination. Recall reverses each original journey from the member's reached point; a redirected journey instead uses its validated homeward route from that reached point. Shorter returns wait for the rest of the party. It never predicts future Core ticks. Previously saved prototype missions without individual routes retain their shared-route semantics. Pause, camera changes, frame rate, culling and hidden tabs cannot advance a mission or reveal terrain. Saving during a visual fade preserves committed knowledge. A route replacement resets interpolation at the authoritative reached position, so an old route distance cannot be applied to a new path. Discovery refreshes seasonal foliage alone, preserving the existing terrain materials and live snow uniforms. Disposal restores original terrain colors and shader hooks. Scenery updates cannot repeatedly multiply the seasonal tint, accumulate snow shader wrappers or leave Winter updates attached to abandoned uniforms.

## Save/load and headless replay

Core JSON saves individual routes (including dispatch-time origins), target, membership, provision charge, policy, phase, distance, return-start progress and timestamps. Redirected missions also retain original home origins and their bounded planned homeward routes; these undergo the same topology, dry-ground and slope checks on import. No renderer is needed to continue an already-dispatched journey. The authoritative blueprint is reattached by `FjordCampaign` after the existing versioned geography is regenerated. A bound geography context is mandatory to advance an active physical mission. Core-only tooling must provide the same context explicitly; it must not silently advance against unrelated geography. Import checks identity, route topology and canonical walkability before committing. Historical saves without `world.spatial` retain their previous semantics and gain no invented missions.

Departure and return have factual events and participant references. Food provisions appear as spending in the existing Clan Book factual ledger. Mission reports list current phase/outcome and the original charge; no looted resources or fictitious rival encounters are generated by Explore Area.
