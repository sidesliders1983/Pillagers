What changes in this comparison?
  • The same generated basalt source is delivered at a larger authored joint scale. Grass, soil and shore textures retain the previous candidate.
  • Rock retains neutral mineral colour instead of receiving the grass-coloured seasonal multiplier.
  • Existing seasonal snow strength is masked by rock orientation: upward-facing surfaces retain snow; steep walls stay more exposed. No snow is painted into the texture.
  • Both sides are colour studies with terrain normal and roughness maps disabled. Existing terrain normals still provide lighting and slope. No new geometry, snow map, rendering pass or dependency.

This targets material readability. Large mountain silhouettes and additional ledges require a separate procedural geometry study. The existing heightmap is retained here.
