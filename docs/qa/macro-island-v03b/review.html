Geologically driven island formation

Issue #133 · 13 October 2026 · 800 × 800 m · 2 m canonical heightfield

QA report · Generation measurements · Browser evidence · Geographical research · Open Environment Lab

Elongated basalt accumulation forms the initial uplands. A downhill drainage network drives broad glacial erosion; the sea floods its lower valleys. Ridges are the remaining high ground. Lowland pockets are measured afterwards, without flattening settlement rectangles.

Review the bare form first. All comparisons below use Day lighting, paused water, no fog, no nature and no village. Green → cream indicates increasing elevation; the oblique view uses a neutral material. Click an image for full size. These are bounded procedural approximations, not a physical glacier simulation.
