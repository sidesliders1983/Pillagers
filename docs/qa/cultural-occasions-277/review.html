Wedding celebrations and communal ceremonies

Optional new occasions for the existing campfire gathering prototype. Each costs 3 Food and earns the same single prepared weather assessment. The culture section remains below the campfire lighting controls.

Try a real wedding

Download wedding review campaign

  1. Open the game, then use Menu → Import campaign to select the downloaded file. Export your current campaign first if you want to keep it.
  2. The paused campaign has a genuine new partnership between Thorrik Hakonarson and Leofhild, with 4 Food remaining. Ordinary rules and real farming commands produced this save; it contains no extra Food, forced partnership or previous gathering.
  3. Select the campfire. Under Occasion, choose Wedding celebration, then the couple. Read partnership record should show their real source.
  4. Choose Support gathering, check both names and the 3 Food quote, then try Not now. The partnership and Food must remain unchanged.
  5. Review again and choose Sponsor · 3 Food. Food becomes 1. The relationship keeps its original date; this source cannot be celebrated a second time.
  6. Enable sound and run the clock with the campfire visible. Both partners must actually arrive with at least one guest before the short presentation starts. Pausing freezes it. Long or unavailable routes may skip decoration; they never create or undo the paid Core event.
  7. Use Read last gathering to see both partners and actual guests. Reloading must preserve the record without replaying the payment or performance.

A wedding is optional during the first game year after a new partnership. Founding couples are excluded. Missing the celebration window never ends the partnership.

Try the communal ceremony

Reimport the same untouched review file, or start a new prototype campaign. Select the campfire → Occasion → Communal ceremony. Review, decline and sponsor through the same 3 Food flow. It needs no wedding or invented religion. It adds no faith stock, fertility benefit, weather reroll or separate bonus.

Test the native selectors and buttons on a narrow screen, camera navigation, pause/resume and returning from another app. The existing actor, sound, motion and light budgets are reused.
