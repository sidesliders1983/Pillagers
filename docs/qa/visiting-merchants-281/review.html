Ready-to-trade checkpoints

Supply merchant checkpoint · Craft merchant checkpoint

These physical-island UI fixtures start with 100 Food / 40 Materials, use ordinary Core stepping for two Winters, and perform any equipment crafting through real workers. These QA stocks are not the new-campaign defaults.

  1. Use the start menu's Recover or import → Import campaign → Continue campaign.
  2. Open Settlement → Visiting merchant · View offers, or select your Longship. Arrival also appears in Activity Center.
  3. Review an offer, cancel with Not now, then review and Confirm exchange. Check the exact give/get quantities, remaining lots and stored stocks.
  4. Try selling stored equipment in the Craft checkpoint. Worn items remain protected. No player Longship or travel pack is charged.
  5. Close, reopen, export/reload and continue. Offers and stock should remain unchanged. Start the clock and advance one Winter to see departure.
  6. Try portrait/landscape, scrolling, close, keyboard focus, camera and paused/background return. No automatic purchase occurs.

To test the natural schedule, start a new campaign with the trade link and play two Winters. Ordinary defaults remain 15 Food / 5 Materials. No merchant is guaranteed to solve a shortage.
