How to interpret these measurements

These are different campaigns from the owner's photographed playtest; no export of that run was supplied. All eight seeds are reused across conditions. Changed eligibility changes RNG consumption, so future weather and births can diverge. A smaller opening stock does not guarantee fewer births or more shortage years in every seed.

The maintained-housing policy chooses one quarter woodworkers and assigns the remainder to suited Food roles, retaining two years of upkeep before construction. The herd policy adds capacity-aware assignment; it does not preserve breeding pairs, trade for replacement bulls or cull animals. That policy's herd losses are an important limitation.

Mortality outcomes combine natural and exposure risks; a death cannot be attributed exclusively to exposure. The harness does not alter production defaults, cap herds or add logs to ordinary saves.
