Rules and scope of this preview
  • Permanent snow requires both the top 10% of the existing height range and a surface slope of approximately 14° or less.
  • Its height and slope boundaries are binary: no broad terrain-colour fade. The cap does not depend on the season.
  • Existing camera clearance, weather fog and vegetation remain visible. No exploration overlay is present in these saved fixtures.
  • The current islands peak at 43–46m. This is relative-scale art direction, with actual high-mountain form and altitude-aware vegetation still to be developed.

No new terrain geometry, snow texture, material pass or simulation rule. The baseline is the reviewed rock treatment, with the same colour-only material setup on both sides.
