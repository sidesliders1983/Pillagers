Find your feet, at your own pace

Can six optional Basics steps help players feel oriented before Chapters I–III, while keeping progress clear when they explore out of order, return later, skip, or revisit?

This is a clickable flow model. The resident card, household link, Settlement panel, camera and time controls below simulate existing game surfaces. They do not run the game. Refreshing resets everything.

Basics progress

    Completed steps stay complete when you revisit. Skipping does not pretend that the remaining steps are complete. Basics never changes game time. A fresh campaign starts paused; the time control is your choice.
