What changed, and what remains open

The authored 3m repeat, normalScale 0.45, roughness, palette and masks are unchanged. Only the three Standard Ground054 delivery maps change from 256 to 512. Low stays 128px without normals. The candidate adds 456.7 KiB of encoded maps and about 3 MiB of estimated RGBA8 texture storage; the latter is not measured VRAM.

Close-up sand has slightly finer grain. The improvement is small at normal distance, and far-view fog and mip filtering largely hide it. No conspicuous new tile pattern appears in these stills. Shore composition, rock silhouettes, wet/dry sand and the warmer Northstar atmosphere need their own changes. There is no demonstrated 512px detail limitation requiring a 1024px candidate.
