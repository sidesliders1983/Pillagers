# Controlled lore · ordinal time style 4

New English narrative prose contains no absolute calendar-year numbers. The opening begins with the arrival itself. Later passages use verified events, elapsed years and ordinal Winters, such as `the fifth winter after the founding party arrived`.

Counting is based on calendar-year differences: arrival in 800 is the arrival winter; the boundary at 805 is the fifth winter **after** that arrival. It is not called the sixth winter. Same-year events remain in the same year. Each paragraph owns its source anchor; changing topic order or selecting another approved paragraph cannot change the reference.

If arrival is unavailable, a recorded clan initialization can supply an anchor. Otherwise the saved opening checkpoint, or the observed period, anchors the account explicitly. This does not invent a founding date. The factual overview retains exact calendar dates and source references. Numbers describing people, livestock or upgrades are not calendar dates; exact Food/Materials quantities remain in the factual overview.

New openings and checkpoints use generator/style/template version 4. Save/load accepts earlier versions and preserves published text/provenance. Versions 2 and 3 remain available for historical catalogues; old chapters are never silently rewritten. The five-Winter cadence, Core time, events, RNG and gameplay rules are unchanged.

The separately labelled model-free review is at `docs/qa/lore-ordinal-time/review.html`. Earlier model measurements and style-3 previews remain unchanged. No model rerun or new inference dependency follows from this wording change.
