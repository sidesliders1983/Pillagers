# Local Lore decision · retain the controlled composer

**Owner decision: 2026-10-12.** Retain the model-free controlled composer for the game. The owner's reading of the compared outputs found too little narrative improvement to justify the optional LLM's additional resource use and complexity. Further narrative development should improve the controlled composer itself.

This is a qualitative owner assessment and product decision, not a new numerical preference score or rerun. The original experiment remains evidence for its recorded style/configuration: sixty default-campaign chapters and nine controlled-fixture chapters per Qwen candidate, all outcomes and fallbacks, pinned artifacts/runtime, source audits, hardware counters and interrupted probes. Historical reports keep their original wording and measurement limits.

The optional adapter, chronological queue and reproduction harness remain developer-only research assets. They are not enabled in player routes, do not publish chapters and add no required model download, paid service or inference dependency. The current composer uses ordinal/event-relative time in new prose while preserving exact dates in UI/factual records and retaining already published passages.

Evidence: [recorded comparison](../qa/local-lore/report.md), [strict re-audit](../qa/local-lore/analysis-review-v3.json), [latest wording review](../qa/lore-ordinal-time/review.html), [style contract](PILLAGERS-NARRATIVE-STYLE-V4.md).
