# Fjordside compressed year prototype (#12)

The world begins in 1200 DC. `AnnualCycle` uses a monotonic `performance.now()` clock: 60,000 real milliseconds is one year. The visual/animation movement delta remains capped independently. At the annual boundary, a summary pauses the world; Continue starts the next sixty-second interval. Switching visual modes never resets the clock. A background tab presents one annual summary on its next rendered frame; additional years do not pass until Continue. The prototype does not persist time across a page reload.

Each annual tick adds one to every active persona's age. A resulting age of 60 or above replaces that persona with an independently generated five-year-old. Existing initial profiles already over 60 are replaced at the first annual tick. Replacement seeds are deterministic from the previous identity, year and population slot; this is not inheritance. The population remains ten. The already-loaded Universal Human LOD2 source allows synchronous replacement without another fetch or an empty population slot. Persona identity, profile-card references and movement slots are replaced while the existing slot's rig, geometry and unchanged textures are reused; resources are released when leaving the world.

Survivors reapply their aged DNA through the shared runtime, including child growth and elder morphology. Cached close LODs and in-flight LOD loads use the current age. Spawning reuses the world's deterministic navigation random stream, checking bounds, dry terrain and obstacle clearance, with a safe grid fallback.

The always-visible HUD shows year and averaged FPS. Development tools → Debug offers Time visualization: Off, Day / Night, Seasons. Off retains the baseline static Day/Night presets. Animated modes share the same cycle progress; Day / Night starts at noon, reaches midnight halfway and returns to noon at the tick. Light colour, position, ambient, sky, fog, exposure and window/fire intensity interpolate without changing the light set or shadow map allocation every frame.

Seasons interpolate spring at 0s, summer at 15s, autumn at 30s and winter at 45s, returning to spring at 60s. Terrain/foliage material tints, sky, fog and light warmth change. A shader uniform blends winter snow colour onto terrain and green foliage while preserving dark woody surfaces. No geometry regeneration, environment reload, weather or seasonal gameplay is involved. The debug panel includes year progress, ages and respawn count.

Validation covers the exact 59,999/60,000ms boundary, duplicate calls, missed-frame catch-up, 120 annual population transitions, random safe respawns, periodic visual curves, resource reuse, and restoration of Off/fog settings. `scripts/world-year-cycle-smoke.cjs` drives the real clock through exact boundaries on desktop and emulated mobile, verifies identities/ages/spawns/count, annual transitions with Continue, mode switches and a single body-source fetch. Set `SKIP_CATCHUP=1` for a shorter visual-only rerun. `scripts/world-aging-profiles-smoke.cjs` checks death-card dismissal, child selection and live age updates. Screenshots are stored in ignored `artifacts/`.

Headless software WebGL checks functionality and visuals, not real phone performance. The visible FPS counter is intended for comparing the three modes on the target device. The 60-second pace and preferred visualization remain experimental decisions for the user to evaluate.


Year-transition repair: rig inverse bind matrices are recalculated at the asset origin, detached from world translation/rotation. Annual updates retain garment geometry, unchanged tint textures and animation phase. Replacement personas reuse the slot's existing GPU rig/geometry while updating identity, morphology and safe spawn location. A year-summary modal now pauses the simulation after each annual tick and displays community count and average age. Continue starts a fresh sixty-second interval; time spent in the modal never ages residents. Browser boundary tests continue explicitly between years instead of auto-catching up multiple years across an open summary.
