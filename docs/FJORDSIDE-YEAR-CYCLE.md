# Fjordside compressed year prototype (#12)

The world begins in 1200 DC. `AnnualCycle` uses a monotonic `performance.now()` clock: 60,000 real milliseconds is one year. The visual/animation movement delta remains capped independently. Annual boundaries age residents without a summary popup or automatic pause: movement, encounters and environmental motion continue. Switching visual modes never resets the clock. Missed annual ticks, including time spent in a background tab, catch up on the next rendered frame. Manual Pause excludes held time from the annual clock and freezes residents, encounters and environmental motion; Resume continues the remaining interval. The prototype does not persist time across a page reload.

Each annual tick adds one to every active persona's age. A resulting age of 60 or above replaces that persona with an independently generated five-year-old. Existing initial profiles already over 60 are replaced at the first annual tick. Replacement seeds are deterministic from the previous identity, year and population slot; this is not inheritance. The population remains ten. The already-loaded Universal Human LOD2 source allows synchronous replacement without another fetch or an empty population slot. Persona identity, profile-card references and movement slots are replaced while the existing slot's rig, geometry and unchanged textures are reused; resources are released when leaving the world.

Survivors reapply their aged DNA through the shared runtime, including child growth and elder morphology. Cached close LODs and in-flight LOD loads use the current age. Spawning reuses the world's deterministic navigation random stream, checking bounds, dry terrain and obstacle clearance, with a safe grid fallback.

The always-visible HUD shows year and averaged FPS. Development tools → Debug offers Time visualization: Off, Day / Night, Seasons. Off retains the baseline static Day/Night presets. Animated modes share the same cycle progress; Day / Night starts at midnight on January 1, reaches noon halfway and returns to midnight at the tick. Light colour, position, ambient, sky, fog, exposure and window/fire intensity interpolate without changing the light set or shadow map allocation every frame.

Seasons interpolate winter at 0s, spring at 15s, summer at 30s and autumn at 45s, returning to winter at 60s. Terrain/foliage material tints, sky, fog and light warmth change. A shader uniform blends winter snow colour onto terrain and green foliage while preserving dark woody surfaces. No geometry regeneration, environment reload, weather or seasonal gameplay is involved. The debug panel includes year progress, ages and respawn count.

Validation covers the exact 59,999/60,000ms boundary, duplicate calls, missed-frame catch-up, 120 annual population transitions, random safe respawns, periodic visual curves, resource reuse, and restoration of Off/fog settings. `scripts/world-year-cycle-smoke.cjs` drives the real clock through exact boundaries on desktop and emulated mobile, verifies identities/ages/spawns/count, uninterrupted annual transitions, mode switches and a single body-source fetch. Set `SKIP_CATCHUP=1` for a shorter visual-only rerun. `scripts/world-aging-profiles-smoke.cjs` checks death-card dismissal, child selection and live age updates. Screenshots are stored in ignored `artifacts/`.

Headless software WebGL checks functionality and visuals, not real phone performance. The visible FPS counter is intended for comparing the three modes on the target device. The 60-second pace and preferred visualization remain experimental decisions for the user to evaluate.


Year-transition repair: rig inverse bind matrices are recalculated at the asset origin, detached from world translation/rotation. Annual updates retain garment geometry, unchanged tint textures and animation phase. Replacement personas reuse the slot's existing GPU rig/geometry while updating identity, morphology and safe spawn location. Annual transitions continue without blocking UI. `scripts/qa/check-fjordside-year.mjs` verifies three annual transitions through the visible Fjordside controls with a controlled external wall clock: no modal or automatic pause, continuing resident movement, correct aging, preserved geography, and manual Pause/Resume without aging during the hold. Historical QA reports retain their original summary/Continue measurements.
