Targets use the nearest **complete actual native body surface**, with the same admitted barycentric body anchor for position and native weights. The fit tucks cloth toward body +3 mm under the actual opaque main vest, belt and front tab. Eight-millimetre tangent-neighborhood sampling softens the coverage boundary; a compact 30 mm source-cloth geodesic falloff compresses the connected inner opening seam. The emitted coherent recipe replaces the earlier arbitrary local weight patch. Its derivation is tracked, readable and deterministic; the source manifest pins the exact data hash and geometry inputs.

The uncompressed fit-only binding currently occupies 4,369,149 bytes, including the 4,366,673-byte native fit table. It adds no vertices, triangles, materials, textures or skeletons at runtime.
